# Brand marks

Most of the model vendor logos here come from [lobe-icons](https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons)
(`@lobehub/icons-static-svg`, MIT), normalised to a 24px intrinsic box. Each brand ships twice:

- `<brand>.svg` — the vendor's own colours, for light surfaces (model panel, city directory).
- `<brand>-mono.svg` — the same mark filled white, for the dark glass nameplates on the houses
  and the frosted labels floating over the city.

`fpt.svg` is the exception. It is the FPT Smart Cloud logo, taken from the artwork on
[Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Fpt_smart_cloud.png) and cropped to the
tricolour FPT mark: the full lockup sets "Smart Cloud" beside it on one line, which is unreadable in
a 17px tag. No vector was available, so both variants wrap a small trimmed PNG inside an SVG; the
white one is keyed from the artwork's own ink coverage. Marks are therefore not all square, and
every slot that shows one letterboxes it rather than stretching it.

The marks remain the trademarks of their owners (DeepSeek, Z.ai, Google, Alibaba Qwen, Meta, FPT)
and are used here only to identify which model each district serves.
